# 📊 ESG Risk & Investment Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) scores to identify sustainable and low-risk companies for long-term investment.

## 🎯 Objective
To build an ESG-based scoring approach and evaluate company risk levels.

## 📊 Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib)
- Power BI
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📈 Key Insights
- Average ESG Score: 55.13 (Moderate performance)
- 38% companies fall under high-risk category
- Strong positive relationship between ESG score and profitability
- Finance & Technology sectors lead in ESG performance
- Energy & Transportation sectors show higher risk

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Invest in companies with ESG score > 70
- Focus on Finance, Technology, Healthcare sectors
- Avoid high-risk companies in Energy & Transportation
- Maintain diversified ESG portfolio


## 🧠 Key Takeaway
High ESG performance indicates lower risk and better long-term returns.
